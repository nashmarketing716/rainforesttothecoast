Privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Rainforest to the Coast (rainforesttothecoast.com) is operated by Grays Harbor Tourism in partnership with Westport and Ocean Shores. This page explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you fill out a form, we collect what you enter: name, email, ZIP or postal code, travel timing, interests, and any trip plan you built. We also record how you found the site (campaign tags in the link) and the page you came in on.

How we use it

We use it to send trip ideas, event updates and travel information about Grays Harbor County, and to measure how well our marketing works. We may use hashed email lists to show relevant ads on platforms such as Meta and Google. We don't sell your personal information.

Service providers

Forms are processed by our hosting provider, Netlify. Photos and event listings load from visitgraysharbor.com and tourismoceanshores.com, partner logos from experiencewestport.com, and route links open Google Maps, which have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from any email at any time. To ask us to access or delete your information, email Grays Harbor Tourism at info@visitgraysharbor.com.

Rainforest to the Coast is a partnership of Grays Harbor Tourism, Ocean Shores and Westport, featuring every corner of Grays Harbor County.
