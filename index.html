Ancient rainforest in the morning. Pacific sunset by dinner.

Grays Harbor County is the gateway to the Washington Coast. The Rainforest to the Coast Road Trip links the mossy giants of the Quinault Rain Forest with miles of open Pacific Ocean beaches, less than an hour apart, and the harbor towns in between. Everything is on one easy loop off I-5.

  • Hike among some of the largest trees on Earth
  • Dig razor clams, beachcomb and storm-watch on the coast
  • Fresh-off-the-boat seafood in Westport

Is this your kind of trip?

You want big trees and quiet trails

The Quinault Rain Forest is one of only three temperate rain forests in the Western Hemisphere, and its "Valley of the Giants" is home to the world's largest Sitka spruce.

You want room to breathe on the beach

Driftwood, dunes and surf stretch along the Northern and Southern Beaches. Fly a kite, dig clams, ride a bike on the sand or just watch the storms roll in.

You want real towns, not tourist traps

Working marinas, timber-baron mansions, murals, museums and local distilleries give every stop a story, and a good meal.

The route

Five stops, one loop, no wrong turns

Come in from I-5 on US-12, then follow US-101 and the coast highways. Drive it in a weekend or take a week.

    Port Cities: history and harbor

    Aberdeen, Hoquiam, Cosmopolis. Kurt Cobain's hometown: visit the Kurt Cobain Museum and Kurt Cobain Memorial Park in Aberdeen. Plus timber-baron homes, downtown murals, the 7th Street Theatre, and the Grays Harbor National Wildlife Refuge, where hundreds of thousands of shorebirds stop during migration.

    East County: the gateway

When to go

Every season has its moment

Spring

Shorebird migration fills the Grays Harbor wildlife refuge, and the rainforest is at its greenest.

Summer

Long beach days, canoeing on Lake Quinault, festivals, and cranberry blossoms in late June.

Fall

Cranberry harvest along the Cranberry Coast, fall fishing, and quieter trails.

Winter

Storm-watching, king tides, holiday lights, and the rainforest living up to its name.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Quinault really a rainforest?

Yes. The Quinault Rain Forest is one of only three temperate coniferous rain forests in the Western Hemisphere. It sits at the southern end of Olympic National Park and Olympic National Forest.

How long does the drive take?

You can see the highlights in two days. Three to five days lets you slow down, add the Southern Beaches, and stay overnight in both the rainforest and on the coast. The itineraries above show what fits.

What should I pack?

Layers, rain gear and good walking shoes, even in summer. Bring a camera and binoculars for elk, eagles and shorebirds.

Can I dig razor clams?

Yes, when a dig is open. You'll need a Washington shellfish license, and dig dates and beaches are set by the Washington Department of Fish & Wildlife, so check their razor clam page before you go.

Do I need a pass for the trails?

Some trailheads around Lake Quinault are on national park or national forest land and may need a recreation pass. Check the trailhead signs or the park and forest websites before you go.

Is the route good for kids and dogs?

Yes. Beaches, short forest loops, tide pools and bike rentals all work for families, and many beaches welcome dogs on leash. Check each park's rules.

Where can I fly kites in Grays Harbor?

The wide, flat, windy beaches of Ocean Shores are some of the best kite-flying spots on the Washington Coast, with steady onshore breezes most afternoons. The beaches at Westport Light State Park, Grayland, Pacific Beach and Moclips are good too. Kite flying is free, and the Ocean Shores beach approaches put you right on the sand.

When is razor clam season, and where is the 2026 digging schedule?

Razor clam digs on Grays Harbor beaches are set by the Washington Department of Fish & Wildlife (WDFW). Digs usually run on low tides from fall through spring on Twin Harbors (Westport and Grayland), Copalis and Mocrocks (Ocean Shores north to Moclips). WDFW announces each dig after toxin testing, so check the WDFW razor clam page for current dates. You need a Washington shellfish license.

Where are Lake Wynoochee and Wynoochee Falls?

Lake Wynoochee is a reservoir behind Wynoochee Dam in Olympic National Forest, about 35 miles north of Montesano on Wynoochee Valley Road. Wynoochee Falls is a short hike upstream. Coho Campground, a national forest campground, sits on the lakeshore with swimming, paddling and a trail around the lake. The road is long and partly gravel, so allow extra time.

Is Aberdeen Kurt Cobain's hometown?

Yes. Kurt Cobain grew up in Aberdeen. Visit Kurt Cobain Memorial Park by the Young Street Bridge over the Wishkah River, the Kurt Cobain Museum run by the nonprofit Music Project, and the "Welcome to Aberdeen: Come As You Are" sign on the way into town.

Where can I see the Lady Washington tall ship?

The Lady Washington's home port is Aberdeen, at the nonprofit Grays Harbor Historical Seaport. The ship travels the West Coast for much of the year, so check the Seaport's schedule for dockside tours and sails before you go.

Where is the ghost forest in Washington?

The Copalis ghost forest stands along the Copalis River near Copalis Beach, just north of Ocean Shores. The silver, standing dead cedars were killed when the land dropped and flooded with salt water during the 1700 Cascadia earthquake. It's best seen by kayak from the river at high tide.

Can I still find Japanese glass floats on the beach?

They're rare now, but beachcombers still find glass floats and other treasures on Grays Harbor beaches, especially after winter storms and king tides. Walk the tide line early in the morning on the beaches from Moclips to Grayland.

Are Ocean Shores beaches dog-friendly?

Yes. Dogs are welcome on Ocean Shores and most Grays Harbor beaches on a leash, and the beaches are big enough for long walks. Many places to stay welcome pets; ask about pet policies when you book.

What is there to do in Moclips, Humptulips and Montesano?

Moclips is a quiet beach town at the north end of the Ocean Shores beaches, great for beachcombing and sunsets. Humptulips sits on US-101 on the way to Lake Quinault and is known for salmon and steelhead fishing on the Humptulips River. Montesano is the county seat, with the historic courthouse, Lake Sylvia State Park and the road north to Lake Wynoochee.
